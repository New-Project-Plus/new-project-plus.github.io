Pick-a-Lot privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Pick-a-Lot, made by New Project Plus LLC, does with your information. It covers the app on every platform it's available on.

Information the app collects

None. Pick-a-Lot has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting of its own, and it doesn't use the internet. We never see your dilemmas or anything else you put in the app.

Where your data is stored

Your dilemmas and settings are stored only on your device. If your device backs up apps to your account with your platform, such as Android backup to your Google account or iCloud Backup on Apple devices, that backup may include them, and only you can restore it. A dilemmas file you export goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

Third-party services

The app includes no third-party services that receive data. The store you got the app from may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics if you've allowed your device to share them with app developers; that's controlled in your device's settings and covered by the store's privacy policy, such as Google's or Apple's. Links in the app (this policy, sending feedback) open in your browser or email app.

Children

Pick-a-Lot isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Delete dilemmas in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app (on Android, clearing the app's storage also works). Backups are managed in your device's or account's backup settings.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to answer you and will not share your email with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above.
